Shape your underwater adventure

Choose a route through authored whale, ruins or reef stories. Your decisions change later actions and endings.

What changes in this adventure?

Authored stories, not AI-generated worlds. Whale words select a whale encounter; ruins, temple or ancient select ruins; other underwater descriptions select a reef. Whale takes precedence over ruins. An explicit whale rescue request takes precedence over Exploration focus. Mood and difficulty selections override tone or challenge words in the description. Tense adds a trench-current event; Helping adds an intervention objective; Challenging spends your reserve on direct action, removing the later protection option. Names, treasure, custom creatures and other extra details are not generated. Preferences apply only to underwater stories; fantasy, space and imported files keep their existing stories.

Open a saved story file

Choose a saved adventure from your computer. A valid file starts automatically; you do not need to type its title above or click Generate My Game afterward.

For The Song Beneath Glass Reef, open the project's stories folder and choose song-beneath-glass-reef.story.json. Play sample adventure opens a different story, The Lost Research Beacon.

JSON only, up to 200 KB. Files are read on this device, not uploaded. Starting a valid import replaces the current narrative run.
