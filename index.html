What can I create? Supported examples

These games use predefined, validated content—not live AI generation. Extra details may not change the result.

Choose Create a Platformer:

  • A relaxed space adventure — Alien Ruins with slower drone attacks.
  • A challenging space adventure — Alien Ruins with faster drone attacks.
  • Explore an ice castle — Ice Castle with a Sentinel choice and no shooting.

Choose Create an Interactive Story:

  • Explore the ocean with a whale — calm underwater exploration.
  • Escape an ocean whirlpool with a whale — an underwater emergency.

Platformers recognize whole words such as space, alien, ruins, ice, frozen and castle. Unsupported or tied theme matches use the default platformer.

Relaxed and challenging affect drone attacks, not platforming or story choices. Using both pace preferences gives standard drone pace.
